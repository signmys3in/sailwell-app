# **App Name**: MediAssistant

## Core Features:

- Patient Medical ID and History: Collect the patient's name, date of birth, alcohol usage, smoking, chronic disease, etc. and create medical ID and history of each patient.
- Symptom Selection: Interactive body diagram for users to pinpoint pain areas and dive into specific organs, there should be also a screen during this step to feed in the system with the vital science of the patient.
- Chronic Disease Check: Screen to check and record any pre-existing chronic diseases, which the AI will consider when suggesting medication.
- Drug Suggestion Tool: AI-powered drug suggestion based on symptoms, chronic diseases, and available medical knowledge.
- Country-Specific Drug Names: Dropdown menu to select a country and display the commercial drug name equivalent for each selected country.
- Stock Level Visualization: Visual representation of drug stock levels, with an initial inventory of 20 boxes per drug. Use tooltips to communicate exactly how many units remain.
- Drug Dispensing and Inventory Deduction: Dispense the selected drug and deduct the dispensed quantity from the inventory stock.
- Narcotics Dispensing with Password: Special screen for narcotics dispensing that requires approval from a 'master' and a password (TAMER) for verification.
- Stock Refill: Screen for admins to increase stock level of existing drugs in stock
- Reporting and Analytics: Generate reports on dispensing activities, and the diagnoses produced by the AI-powered parts of the system.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Calming blue (#5DADE2) to inspire trust and tranquility, reflecting the healthcare context.
- Background color: Soft, desaturated light blue (#EBF5FB), providing a clean and peaceful backdrop.
- Accent color: Gentle green (#82E0AA), used for positive affirmations and action confirmations, signaling healing and progress. Complementary to the primary color.
- Font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif with a modern, machined, objective, neutral look suitable for body text and headlines.
- Use clean, minimalist icons representing different symptoms and medical actions, consistent with the app's color scheme.
- Intuitive layout with clear sections for each step (symptom selection, drug suggestion, etc.).
- Subtle transitions and animations to guide the user through the process and provide feedback.
